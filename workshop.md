# 45 minutes, their files, then stop

Do not call this enablement.

## who is in the room

`once-dont-know-how` and `never-dont-know-how` only. Mix orgs if you have to, never mix them with legal-trust. Legal gets their own hour and a smaller room.

Cap at 12. After that people hide.

## what i bring

- 3 prompts that failed on their kind of question last month (redacted)
- 1 prompt that worked
- how to see *why* it answered (even if that UI is ugly)
- where to send "this is wrong" so it is not a void

I do not bring a slide called "what is an LLM".

## clock

| min | |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | they open the tool. half the room will have the wrong link. |
| 5–15 | they paste a real question from this week. i walk, i do not present. |
| 15–25 | we break one bad answer on purpose. |
| 25–35 | they try a second question. |
| 35–45 | 3 rules i actually want them to remember, written on a wall not a pdf. |

The three rules I use:

1. If you cannot name the source, do not paste it into a memo.
2. If it concerns a person, a deal, or money, a human still sends it.
3. "I don't know" is a valid output. Retrying 8 times is not research.

## after

One slack message to the people who showed up, with *their* example, not a newsletter. Champions get the names of people who looked stuck.

If nobody from an org shows twice, stop inviting them and go talk to their manager about the blocker instead. That is usually trust or data, not calendar.
